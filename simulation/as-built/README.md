# As-Built Simulation

## Overview
After getting all the parts I measured and weighed everything 
with a rinky dinky ruler and a kitchen scale. I didn't anticipate 
how time consuming it would be but I got it done and updated 
the OpenRocket simulation with the actual numbers.

## What Changed From Pre-Build

The biggest changes from the pre-build were the nose cone being 
way longer than I expected and the electronics being way heavier 
than I thought. This made the rocket heavier overall.

| Metric | Pre-Build | As-Built |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Stability | 1.64 cal | 1.80 cal |
| Apogee | 586 ft | 518 ft |
| Velocity off rod | 61.4 ft/s | 58.2 ft/s |
| Total mass with motor | 193g | 218g |

## What I Learned

Even though the rocket was heavier the apogee dropped from 
586ft to 518ft because of the extra weight but stability 
improved because of the nose cone pushing the center of 
gravity forward.

This simulation alone is teaching me so much about engineering. 
You can estimate all you want but actually doing it will tell 
you the truth for sure. Updating your models with real measured 
data is not optional.
